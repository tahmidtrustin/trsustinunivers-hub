Masterclass & Frameworks

Content Creation & Audience Growth Guide

Full end-to-end guidance from video scripting to algorithm monetization.

1

YouTube Scriptwriting & Hook Psychology

The first 5 seconds determine 80% of video retention. Learn how to structure high-performing hooks using the Hook-Story-Offer formula.

The 3-Second Rule:

State a high-stakes outcome or challenge instantly. Avoid long intro logos or generic welcomes.

2

Video Editing Workflow (Premiere, CapCut, DaVinci)

Standardize your post-production workflow to cut editing time in half while maintaining cinema-quality pacing.

Editing Timeline Order:

A-Roll Rough Cut $\rightarrow$ Audio Normalization (-14 LUFS) $\rightarrow$ Sound Effects (SFX) $\rightarrow$ B-Roll & Text Animations.

3

Thumbnail Design & Click-Through Rate (CTR)

Thumbnails account for 90% of a viewer's decision to click. Learn visual hierarchy, facial expression contrast, and color balance.

High-CTR Rule:

Limit thumbnail text to 3 words or fewer. Complement the title without repeating the title verbatim.

4

Short-Form Virality (Shorts, Reels, TikTok)

Algorithmic guidelines to consistently reach 100k+ views across vertical video networks.

Vertical Video Blueprint:

Maintain dynamic text captions on screen at all times. Change visuals or camera angles every 2.5 seconds.
